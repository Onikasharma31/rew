About Us

Driven by Precision. Defined by Integrity.

Rajesh Engineering Works is a dynamic, multi-disciplinary engineering firm with a strong reputation in the field of sheet metal fabrication and Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) manufacturing. Founded in 1997, we have grown to become a trusted partner for both public and private sector clients, delivering high-quality engineering solutions with precision and reliability.

Who We Are

With decades of hands-on experience, we specialize in the design, fabrication, and manufacturing of a wide range of sheet metal components including:

  • E-rickshaw chassis
  • Electric panel boxes
  • Engine canopies
  • Server racks
  • Custom sheet metal structures

Our in-house capabilities and skilled team allow us to take on both small- and large-scale fabrication projects with uncompromising quality standards.

ETP Plant Experts

For over 20 years, Rajesh Engineering Works has been at the forefront of Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) design, manufacturing, and commissioning—serving clients across India and internationally in countries such as South Africa, Congo, Nepal, and Bangladesh.
Our portfolio includes municipal and industrial projects for wastewater treatment and water purification, where we deliver end-to-end solutions that meet environmental and regulatory standards.

Technology & Expertise

We combine traditional engineering knowledge with state-of-the-art technology to deliver superior results. Our use of Computer-Aided Design and Drafting (CADD) systems enhances design accuracy, improves project efficiency, and ensures seamless collaboration between our engineering teams and clients.

This approach helps us:

  • Increase production quality
  • Reduce operational costs
  • Accelerate project timelines
  • Deliver high-value, sustainable solutions

Rajesh Engineering Works stands for innovation, integrity, and excellence in engineering. Whether it's precision sheet metal fabrication or complex ETP systems, we are committed to delivering the highest quality products and services to meet your needs.
